## 4. Rule Version, Known Limitations, Migration & Recovery Guide (`BCS-07.3`)

### 4.1 Rule Version & Contract Provenance
- **Active Rule Version:** `bcs-phase1-local-v1` (`ReportingMetricService::RULE_VERSION`).
- Every `/api/reports/bite-summary` response, `BiteReportSnapshot` record (`rule_version` column), and A4 meeting print pack embeds `bcs-phase1-local-v1` alongside `period_start`, `period_end`, `cutoff`, `date_basis`, and `privacy_mode`.

### 4.2 Known Limitations & Report-Owner Validation Notes
1. **Local DOH Form Validation (`D01`–`D07`):**
   - The redesign reconciles 1-for-1 against the three confirmed `Reports & Analytics > DOH Submissions` forms (`Rabies Exposure Registry`, `ABTC Monthly Report`, and `Cohort Report (Quarterly)`).
   - Outputs are labeled as **clinic program summary and meeting pack** figures reconciled to the application's 3 DOH templates. Before labeling external exports "DOH-certified," the RHU report owner should confirm any regional FHSIS / DOH Northern Mindanao manual updates on items `D02`–`D07` (documented in [Phase 1 Package](./bite_cases_summary_phase_1.md)).
2. **Human Rabies Surveillance (`HR01`):**
   - Because the clinical module does not include a verified human rabies death/case investigation registry, `human_rabies_cases` always reports `null` (`N/A — not tracked in clinic module`) rather than an unverified `0`.
3. **External Referral Outcomes:**
   - Episodes transferred/referred to another ABTC (e.g., NMMC) display `Transferred/Referred; treatment elsewhere: Unknown unless verified` unless an explicit verified external treatment record (`is_external = true`) is recorded.
4. **Exposure Geography vs. Residence Geography:**
   - Prevention and hotspot tables default to **Place of Exposure** (`bite_place`) and never substitute the patient's home barangay when `bite_place` is blank (blank exposure locations are reported under `Unknown exposure location`). Staff can switch to **Patient Residence** (`geography_basis=residence`) for patient follow-up planning.

### 4.3 Database Migrations & Rollback / Recovery Steps
Two additive migrations support the Bite Cases Summary redesign:
1. `backend/database/migrations/2026_11_14_150000_add_bite_date_is_provisional_to_bite_incidents_table.php`
   - Adds `bite_date_is_provisional` (`boolean`, default `false`, indexed) to `bite_incidents`.
2. `backend/database/migrations/2026_11_14_180000_create_bite_report_snapshots_and_actions_tables.php`
   - Creates `bite_report_snapshots` (immutable/versioned meeting snapshot payloads) and `bite_report_actions` (clinic planning action register).

**Apply migrations:**
```powershell
cd backend
php artisan migrate
```

**Rollback / recovery procedure (if needed in local/staging environments):**
```powershell
cd backend
php artisan migrate:rollback --step=2
```
- Rolling back step 1 drops `bite_report_actions` and `bite_report_snapshots` without affecting clinical patient, incident, consultation, or vaccination tables.
- Rolling back step 2 drops `bite_date_is_provisional` from `bite_incidents` without deleting any incident rows.

### 4.4 Operator Guidance for Clinic Staff & Report Owners
1. **Preparing a Monthly or Quarterly Meeting Report:**
   - Navigate to **Bite Cases Summary** (`/bite-cases`).
   - Select the reporting period (**Month**, **Quarter**, **Year**, or **Custom**), **Cutoff Date**, and **Privacy Mode** (**Internal Aggregate** for RHU staff review or **Conference / Safe (<5)** for external/LGU presentations).
   - Review the **Scope Banner** at the top to confirm the period, cutoff, total recorded episodes, and whether the period is marked **Partial period**.
2. **Reviewing the Three Focused Tabs:**
   - **Tab 1 — Overview:** Review the 6 core KPIs, the 5-stage **Patient Journey Stage Matrix**, Exposure Category I–III vs. Unassessed breakdown, Demographics (`Male`/`Female`, `<15`/`15+`), Animal Profile, and Top Exposure Locations. Click **View Case List** in the header (authorized staff only, when not in Conference mode) to inspect individual episodes in a slide-over drawer.
   - **Tab 2 — Treatment & Follow-up:** Review PEP initiation (`new_course_started / new_course_prescribed`), Mature Cohort Completion (`completed_mature_courses / mature_started_courses`), Biologics (`ERIG`, `HRIG`, `RIG Indicated without Verified Receipt`), Overdue Follow-up (`overdue_episodes`, `overdue_patients`, `outstanding_dose_slots`), and the **Cross-Form Reconciliation & Data Quality** accordions.
   - **Tab 3 — Planning & Actions:** Log concrete barangay or clinic interventions in the **Action Register**, save a **Draft Snapshot**, **Finalize (Lock)** the snapshot for a meeting, or **Save Revision (`v2+`)** if late records arrive after finalization.
3. **Printing / Presenting:**
   - Click **Present** for the 6-slide full-screen meeting deck, or **Print Pack** to open the A4 portrait Meeting Pack (`/print/reports/bite-summary`) for browser print / Save-as-PDF.
